# Running GhostPilot Inside Antigravity IDE ($0 Using Gemini 3.8 Flash)

You do **not** need to pay for OpenAI, Claude, or any extra Gemini API billing to run GhostPilot on autopilot. Follow this 3-step loop inside Antigravity IDE:

---

## Step 1: Set Up Your Niche & Whop/Affiliate Offer (Once)
1. Start GhostPilot (`npm run dev`) and open `http://localhost:3000`.
2. Click **Brand & Offers** in the left sidebar.
3. Pick a niche preset (e.g., *High-Ticket Sales*, *AI Tools*, *Real Estate*, *Whop Digital Products*) or type your own niche + subreddits.
4. Add your Whop, Gumroad, or affiliate offer link, promise, and giveaway keyword (e.g., `CLOSE`).

---

## Step 2: Mine Fresh Niche Intelligence ($0)
1. Click **Authority Research** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Scan Live Niche Radar ($0)** to pull fresh Reddit complaints, Google News stories, GitHub repos, and Hacker News discussions.
3. Click **Compile 5-Angle Dossier**.

---

## Step 3: Let Antigravity (Gemini 3.8 Flash) Write Your Entire Week
1. Click **Antigravity Bridge** in the sidebar.
2. Select **Full 7-Day Autopilot Pack** and click **Create Task Pack**.
   - GhostPilot writes all your niche context, persona, radar data, and offers into `antigravity-sync/tasks.json`.
3. Click **Copy Prompt for Antigravity**.
4. Paste that prompt into your **Antigravity IDE chat** (with Gemini 3.8 Flash selected).
   - Antigravity reads `antigravity-sync/tasks.json` and writes your finished week of posts, PDF lead magnet, and carousel into `antigravity-sync/results.json`.
5. Go back to **Antigravity Bridge** in your browser and click **Sync Results from Antigravity**.
   - GhostPilot automatically scores every tweet (0–100), drops winners into your open weekly time slots, and schedules Auto-Plugs!

---

## Optional Power-Up: Install `morluto/rea` Reverse-Engineering Agents

Want Antigravity to deeply reverse-engineer any competitor funnel or niche playbook before writing your tweets? Run this once in your Antigravity terminal:

```bash
npx rea-agents setup
```

Then ask Antigravity:
> "Use the reverse-engineering agents to inspect our latest `antigravity-sync/tasks.json` dossier, map out the highest-converting giveaway funnel for our Whop offer, and write the finished posts + PDF into `antigravity-sync/results.json`."
