# Anti-AI-Slop Humanizer Skill

Run this audit on EVERY tweet, thread, and PDF section before saving.

## Banned AI Vocabulary (Delete Immediately)
`delve`, `tapestry`, `testament`, `beacon`, `realm`, `paramount`, `meticulously`, `navigate the landscape`, `pivotal`, `seamlessly`, `robust`, `foster`, `harness`, `unleash`, `revolutionize`, `supercharge`, `synergy`, `cutting-edge`, `ever-evolving`, `game-changer`, `unlock the power`, `elevate your`, `embark on a journey`, `let's dive in`, `in today's fast-paced world`.

## Banned Structural Tropes
1. **Binary Contrast Spam**: Never write `"It's not just X, it's Y"` or `"This isn't about X — it's about Y"`. State the direct truth instead.
2. **Em-Dash Spam**: Maximum 1 em-dash (`—`) per post. Use periods and line breaks for punchy pacing.
3. **Hashtag Clutter**: Zero hashtags (`#AI #Growth`) at the end of X posts — they look automated and hurt distribution.
4. **Stock Emoji Spam**: Max 1 functional emoji (`🧵`, `👇`) when needed — never stack `🚀🔥💡✨`.
5. **Fake Objections**: Do not write `"You might be thinking..."` unless naming a real buyer objection from Reddit/customer research.

## Voice Calibration
- Replace vague claims (`save time`) with concrete specifics (`cut 4 hours of manual research down to 90 seconds`).
- Mix short 4-word punchlines with 12-word tactical explanations.
